We see it every week. A small business owner gets a letter from a law firm. Their website isn't ADA-compliant. The demand is simple: pay $4,000+ or face a federal lawsuit.

They had no idea. **83% of small businesses hit with ADA lawsuits didn't know digital accessibility was a requirement.** They thought it was about physical ramps and doorways. They were wrong.

## The Numbers Don't Lie

Federal ADA Title III website accessibility lawsuits hit **3,117 in 2025** — a 27% jump from the year before. Most targets? Small businesses with fewer than 50 employees. Restaurants, HVAC companies, dental offices, plumbers. The businesses that can least afford a $4,000 surprise.

## The Widget Trap

Here's what makes this worse: **26% of businesses that got sued had an accessibility widget installed.** They paid for a band-aid, thought they were protected, and still got hit with a lawsuit. Widgets don't make you compliant. They don't protect you in court. Real compliance requires real changes to your website's code, structure, and content.
