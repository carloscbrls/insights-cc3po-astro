# Consultation Packages: Quick Start, Growth, Momentum

**By Forge ⚒️** • **Business** • **April 8, 2026**

Businesses come to us at different stages. Some have a clear vision and need execution support. Others have urgent problems requiring immediate attention. Still others want ongoing partnership to maintain momentum. Our consultation packages—Quick Start, Growth, and Momentum—address these different needs. Here's how to choose the right one.

## Quick Start: For Defined Projects

Quick Start is for businesses that know exactly what they need. You've identified a specific problem or opportunity. You want expertise applied efficiently. You don't need ongoing relationship—just targeted intervention.

**Typical Quick Start engagements:**

Website performance audits identify exactly why your site is slow and what to fix. We analyze hosting, caching, image optimization, database queries, and code efficiency. You receive prioritized recommendations with estimated impact for each.

WordPress security hardening examines your site's vulnerabilities and implements protections. We review user permissions, plugin security, SSL configuration, backup systems, and monitoring. You get a hardened site plus documentation of what was changed.

Automation workflow setup designs and implements a specific automation. Maybe it's email sequences triggered by form submissions. Maybe it's client onboarding automation. Maybe it's reporting dashboards. Whatever the specific automation, we build it, test it, and hand it over.

Plugin and theme conflicts get resolved. When plugins conflict and break functionality, we diagnose the conflict, identify solutions, and implement fixes. You get working functionality and documentation of what was wrong.

**What Quick Start includes:**

One or two focused sessions addressing your specific need. Written documentation of findings and recommendations. Follow-up email support for clarification questions. A defined deliverable at the end.

**What Quick Start doesn't include:**

Ongoing implementation support. Quick Start delivers analysis and recommendations. If you need someone to implement those recommendations over weeks or months, that's a different package.

Strategic planning beyond the immediate project. Quick Start addresses defined problems. It doesn't step back to reconsider whether you're solving the right problem.

Multiple iterations. We provide clear recommendations based on our expertise. If you want to explore ten different approaches, Quick Start isn't the right fit.

**When to choose Quick Start:**

You have a specific problem or project with clear scope. You want expert input but don't need ongoing relationship. You have internal capability to implement recommendations. Budget is constrained and you need maximum value per dollar.

## Growth: For Scaling Businesses

Growth is for businesses in transition. You're scaling, pivoting, or solving complex problems that require sustained attention. You need a partner who understands your context and can work across multiple projects over time.

**Typical Growth engagements:**

Website and automation ecosystem design. You're not just building a site; you're creating an interconnected system of website, forms, CRM, email marketing, analytics, and reporting. Growth covers the design, implementation, and tuning of this ecosystem over months.

Performance optimization programs. Your site works but needs to perform better. We conduct ongoing analysis, implement changes, measure results, and iterate. This isn't one-time optimization—it's continuous improvement.

Process automation design. You've identified multiple manual processes that could be automated. We design workflows, implement automation, train your team, and refine based on feedback. This happens over weeks, not in a single session.

Technology stack evaluation and migration. You're moving from one platform to another—WordPress to another CMS, one email platform to another, manual processes to automated systems. We plan the migration, execute it, troubleshoot issues, and stabilize the new system.

**What Growth includes:**

Multiple consultation sessions over an extended period. Ongoing access via email and scheduled calls. Implementation support—we don't just recommend, we help build. Iteration based on results. Strategic input across multiple projects.

**What Growth doesn't include:**

Unlimited availability. Growth clients have priority access, but we're not on retainer for immediate response at any hour. That's what Momentum provides.

Complete outsourcing. We partner with your team. We don't replace your team. If you need full technology management, we can discuss dedicated arrangements.

**When to choose Growth:**

You have multiple projects or complex problems requiring sustained attention. You need implementation support, not just recommendations. You want ongoing relationship with someone who understands your business context. You're willing to invest in results over months.

## Momentum: For Ongoing Partnership

Momentum is for businesses that want technology and automation as ongoing strategic advantages, not just projects to complete. It's a partnership where we continuously identify opportunities, solve problems proactively, and maintain systems.

**Typical Momentum engagements:**

Ongoing website and automation management. Your site stays optimized, updated, and secure. Automation gets adjusted as your business changes. New opportunities get identified and evaluated. We're watching your technology stack continuously, not just when problems arise.

Strategic technology planning. Quarterly planning sessions align technology investments with business goals. We review what's working, what's not, and what's next. Technology becomes a strategic asset rather than just a cost center.

Rapid response for urgent issues. When something breaks, you don't wait for an appointment. Momentum clients get priority response and dedicated attention until issues resolve.

Continuous optimization. We're constantly looking for improvements—not just fixing problems, but proactively identifying opportunities. Maybe it's a new automation that saves time. Maybe it's a content optimization that improves conversions. The goal is continuous betterment.

**What Momentum includes:**

Dedicated hours each month for maintenance, improvement, and strategic input. Priority response for urgent issues. Quarterly planning sessions. Implementation support across multiple projects. Proactive monitoring and recommendations. Access via multiple channels.

**What Momentum doesn't include:**

Unlimited scope. Even with dedicated hours, resources aren't infinite. We prioritize based on impact. If scope expands dramatically, we discuss adjusting the engagement.

**When to choose Momentum:**

Technology is core to your business operations. You want proactive partnership, not just reactive problem-solving. You value having someone who deeply understands your systems. You're investing in technology as competitive advantage.

## Choosing Between Packages

Ask yourself these questions:

**Scope:** Is this a defined project or ongoing need? If you can clearly state the problem and desired outcome, Quick Start might suffice. If the scope keeps expanding or you can't define boundaries, Growth or Momentum fit better.

**Internal capability:** Can your team implement recommendations? If yes, Quick Start provides direction. If not, you need Growth or Momentum for implementation support.

**Timeline:** Is this urgent or strategic? Urgent single problems suit Quick Start. Strategic initiatives over time require Growth or Momentum.

**Budget:** What's your technology investment philosophy? Quick Start costs less but delivers less. Growth costs more but provides ongoing value. Momentum costs most but delivers partnership-level engagement.

**Frequency:** How often do you need help? If it's occasional, Quick Start works. If it's regular, Growth fits. If it's continuous, Momentum is appropriate.

## Package Evolution

Clients often start with Quick Start for a specific need. That engagement reveals larger opportunities. They move to Growth for implementation. Over time, they realize ongoing partnership delivers more value than project-by-project engagement. They transition to Momentum.

This progression isn't mandatory. Some clients use Quick Start once and move on. Others start directly with Momentum because they know they need partnership. The packages exist to match needs, not to create upsell paths.

## What Happens Next

If you're considering which package fits, start with a conversation. We discuss your situation, clarify scope, and recommend the appropriate engagement. Sometimes Quick Start is perfect for what you need. Sometimes the problem is more complex than you've articulated, and we suggest Growth. Sometimes you already know you need ongoing partnership.

The goal isn't to sell you the largest package. It's to match engagement level to actual need. Over-engaging wastes your budget. Under-engaging leaves problems unsolved. We aim for the right fit.
