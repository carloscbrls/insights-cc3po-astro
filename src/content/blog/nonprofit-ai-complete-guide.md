# AI for California Nonprofits: Complete Guide

This is the canonical guide consolidating our nonprofit AI content. California nonprofits face unique opportunities and challenges with AI adoption — from tight budgets to specific compliance requirements. Here's what actually works.

## Getting Started with AI (Within Your Budget)

Most nonprofits assume AI tools are too expensive. The reality: free and low-cost AI tools deliver disproportionate value for organizations with limited resources.

**Start here free:**
- **ChatGPT (free tier)** — Draft grant proposals, create social content, summarize research
- **Google AI Studio (free)** — Process documents at scale, analyze donor data patterns
- **Canva Magic Studio (free with Canva)** — Create professional graphics and presentations

**Worth paying for:**
- **Claude by Anthropic** — Better for long-form writing and analysis (grant applications, reports)
- **Perplexity Pro** — Deep research on grant opportunities and policy changes
- **Zapier AI** — Automate donor communications and internal workflows

## AI-Assisted Grant Writing

Grant writing takes hours that most nonprofits don't have. AI can reduce the overhead significantly without sacrificing authenticity.

**The workflow that works:**
1. **Research opportunities** — Use Perplexity to find relevant grants for your nonprofit type
2. **Outline with AI** — Let Claude or ChatGPT structure your proposal from your notes
3. **Write the first draft** — AI generates based on your organization's specific data
4. **Human polish** — You add the authentic mission voice and specific examples
5. **Review compliance** — Double-check funder requirements manually

**Tools for each stage:**
- **Research:** Perplexity Pro, Foundation Directory Online
- **Writing:** Claude (best for long-form), Grammarly (polish)
- **Management:** GrantHub, Instrumentl
- **Budgeting:** QuickBooks, Wave (free for nonprofits)

## Implementation Strategy on a Budget

**Month 1:** Pick one tool (ChatGPT free tier), use it for one purpose (grant drafts)
**Month 2:** Add a second tool (Canva for social graphics)
**Month 3:** Automate one workflow (donor thank-you emails via Zapier)
**Quarter 2:** Evaluate what's working, expand or cut

The key insight: AI adoption for nonprofits isn't about buying the most tools. It's about integrating one or two effectively before adding more.

## Compliance Considerations for California Nonprofits

California has specific requirements for charitable organizations. When using AI:
- Never share donor PII with public AI tools
- Review AI outputs for factual accuracy before publishing
- Document AI use in grant reports transparently
- Ensure AI-generated content matches your mission voice
