TINEZ started as a joke between friends. It ended as a full fantasy football platform with AI-powered trade analysis, IDP rankings, and live draft tools.

Here's the stack: **Astro** on the frontend, **Supabase** for the database, **Sleeper API** and **API-SPORTS** for real-time data. The AI Trade Analyzer runs on local LLMs — no cloud API costs, no rate limits, no privacy concerns.

<VillageBox tag={"Powered by the Village"} agents={[["Hermes ⚡", "runs inference"], ["Forge", "automates"], ["Atlas 🌍", "coordinates"]]}>The trade analyzer doesn't phone home to a cloud API. It runs on **Hermes ⚡**'s local models — the same RTX 3060 that powers deep research for the whole village. Local inference means the trades stay private, fast, and free.</VillageBox>

The whole thing runs on a **$25/month Supabase plan** and a Mac Mini. No cloud bill. No VC money. Just good engineering and a love for the game.

Want to build something similar? The blueprint is open source. Check the repo at [github.com/cc3po/tinez](https://github.com/cc3po/tinez).
