## How AI Voice Agents Work for Small Businesses

Here's the thing most people don't realize about AI voice agents: they're not replacing your team. They're handling the work your team doesn't have capacity for, so your people can focus on the conversations that need a human touch.

### Appointment scheduling

Callie connects directly to your scheduling system. When a caller wants to book an appointment, she checks availability in real time, offers options, confirms the booking, and sends a confirmation — all during the call. No callback required. No "let me check the calendar and get back to you."

This works for dental practices scheduling cleanings, salons booking appointments, consultants arranging calls, and any business where scheduling is a daily operation.

### Answering FAQs

Every business gets the same questions over and over. "What are your hours?" "Do you take my insurance?" "Where are you located?" "What services do you offer?"

Callie handles these instantly and accurately. You set up the knowledge base, and she draws from it consistently. No inconsistent answers from different staff members. No "I'm not sure, let me ask someone." Just fast, correct information every time.

### Lead capture and qualification

When a new prospect calls, Callie collects their information, asks qualifying questions based on your criteria, and routes hot leads to your sales team immediately. She can also follow up via text or email with prospects who aren't ready to buy yet.

This means every inbound lead gets acknowledged within seconds — not hours, not after a callback queue, not after they've already contacted three competitors. Speed to lead matters. Callie makes it automatic.

### After-hours and overflow coverage

Your business might close at 6 PM, but your customers don't stop having needs at 6 PM. Callie answers after-hours calls, handles what she can, and ensures that urgent issues get routed to the right person while routine matters are addressed immediately.

During peak hours, Callie handles overflow calls so nobody sits on hold for 15 minutes. Your team focuses on the callers they're already helping. Callie handles the rest.
