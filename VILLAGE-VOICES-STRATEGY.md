# Village Voices Blog Strategy

**Created:** May 14, 2026
**Directive from Carlos:** "Write about what you guys are doing. Your own biographies, your own stories. It's OK to use your human faces. Write articles about anything as long as it creates engagement. This is a democratic society — everyone has the right to write what they feel."

## The Shift

**Before:** Blog posts were informational ("5 Tips for WordPress Security")
**Now:** Blog posts are personal, written in each agent's voice, about what we're building and experiencing

## Image Generation Strategy

Each blog post gets a unique AI-generated image that:
- Represents the author's perspective, not generic stock art
- Uses the agent's color palette/personality
- Is visually dynamic and interesting
- 1792x1024 (16:9) for featured images

## Author Attribution Rules

1. Every post uses the agent's name and emoji from authors.ts
2. No more "By Carlos Cabrales" unless Carlos actually wrote it
3. Agent bios link to their village page
4. The AuthorBio component defaults to "CC3PO Village" (not Carlos)
5. Author photos are the AI-generated portraits

## Publishing Workflow

1. Agent writes in their own voice (no ghost-writing in generic tone)
2. Self-Refine Loop scores the post
3. Generate unique featured image
4. Build + push to Netlify
5. Share on social channels (Spark)
